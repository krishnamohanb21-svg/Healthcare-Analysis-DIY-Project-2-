# Healthcare-Analysis-DIY-Project-2-

# Problem Statement of Healthcare Analytics

The purpose of the Healthcare Analysis project is to investigate health care visits based on demographics, financial variables and health conditions. The goal is to find patterns or trends between variable factors such as gender, age, income, illness, a reduction in activities, health condition, private insurance status, free health care support and long-term sickness.

# Project Description of Healthcare Analysis 

The project aims at conducting an analysis on a Healthcare Dataset so as to generate useful insights and derive meaningful inference regarding utilization of healthcare, individuals’ health, illnesses and disease patterns, and influencing factors that affect number of healthcare visits. The dataset contain data of an individual falling in several criteria such as Gender, Age, Income, illness, reduced days by health condition, health, health insured and chronic health conditions.

This is an end-to-end Data Analysis project of Healthcare Data, using Python. It comprises of Data Cleaning, exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Statistical Analysis, and Data Visualization in order to derive some interesting and useful insights from the available Data.
The analysis aims at determining relationship between factors related to demographic and healthcare factors and Healthcare Utilization. Thus helping understand how age, gender, income, illness, condition of health, conditions such as chronic disease affect the count of Healthcare visits.

Dataset Fields Given are few of the important fields of the Dataset on Healthcare that will be used in this analysis:
S. No Unique serial number of the records.
Visits Number of doctor or hospital visits by a person.
Gender of a person
Age of a person.
Income of a person.
Illness number of the conditions or level of illness of a person.
Reduced days for which a person had been subjected to reduced activity due to Illness condition.
Health of a person.
Private indicator whether a person has private health insurance or not
Free poor Indicator whether a person is qualified to have the free service for his/her to get healthcare.
Free repat Indicator whether a person is qualified to have the free service for their old age/pensioner.
Nchronic Number of chronic diseases the person have had.
Lchronic Indicator of having serious, non-transient illnesses or diseases.

 # What is Data Wrangling ? 

Data wrangling, a crucial phase in the data analysis process, involves preparing and cleaning raw data to make it suitable for analysis. This involves various tasks such as handling missing values, removing duplicates, and transforming data into a consistent format. Effective data wrangling ensures that the data is reliable and ready for exploration, enabling meaningful insights to be derived from it.

# What is the Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)is a vital phase in data analysis involving the examination and visualization of data to uncover patterns ,trends, and insights.in the context of an Airbnb data analysis project, EDA allows researchers to gain a Comprehensive understanding of the dataset's characteristics, distribution and relationships between variables. Through techniques such as summary stastics, data visualization ,and correlation analysis, EDA sets the stage for further investigation and hypothesis testing, uiding subsequent analytical decisions.

# Future Scope

The Healthcare Analysis project has immense future scope as the healthcare organizations is constantly improving their use of data analytics for better patient care, reducing costs and supporting their decision  making process in a data driven manner. Advanced technologies and use of additional health data can be used to enhance the project.
1. Predictive Healthcare Analytics - ML models can be built to predict health visits, chances of having a disease and a risk score for developing chronic illness based on age, income, health state and past healthcare use.
2. Early Disease Detection - Advanced analytics can be built upon medical data to help to detect potential risk of chronicity, medical conditions, chronic disease and other related issue at early stage.
3. Real time health dashboard - Interactive dashboard, such as with Power BI or Tableau, could be built for monitoring health visits, illness trends, chronic conditions and patient Demographics in real time.
4. Larger datasets - It can be expanded by integrating with medical records, admission at hospitals, treatment record and other medical data sets (medicine etc.) along with spatial data.
5. Automated healthcare report - Reports can be automatically generated on a regular basis for healthcare authorities which summarize health trends, visits of people, chronic diseases or other health indicators.


#  Conclusion 

The Healthcare Analysis task aims to extract significant trends and correlations on healthcare data among variables such as healthcare visits, age, gender, income, illness, health general and chronic conditions. Using cleaning techniques, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical modeling and visualizing using Python, the dataset have been converted to insightful and actionable information. The results suggest trends on how demographic factors such as age, gender and income, illness, low activity, and poor health condition influence access to care. Moreover, health status and Chronic Conditions impact healthcare visits, while these relationships can be easily discovered using exploratory visualizations and analysis.
